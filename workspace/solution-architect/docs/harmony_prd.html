Section 01

Product Overview

Harmony Physio Platform is a bespoke clinical web application built for Harmony Physiotherapy Clinic (harmonyphysio.co.uk). It replaces fragmented third-party SaaS tools with a single, owned platform covering patient booking, telehealth, clinical records, billing, and secure communications — all hosted within UK data residency boundaries and compliant with GDPR, NHS DTAC, and DSPT requirements.

Section 02

User Personas

🧑‍⚕️

Physiotherapist / Clinician

Primary clinical user

  • Write and access SOAP notes quickly during sessions
  • Start telehealth calls without leaving the platform
  • View patient history, documents, and treatment plans
  • Upload clinical documents and manage consent
🧑‍💼

Admin / Receptionist

Operations user

  • Manage appointment calendar and rescheduling
  • Generate invoices and track payment status
  • Handle incoming booking requests
  • Manage patient records and onboarding
🧑‍🦽

Patient

External end user

  • Book, reschedule, or cancel appointments online
  • Attend telehealth sessions via browser (no app required)
  • View appointment history and upcoming sessions
  • Access and download clinical notes and documents
  • Complete digital consent forms
🔐

Platform Owner / IG Lead

Compliance & security

  • Access audit logs for GDPR/CQC inquiries
  • Manage staff roles and access permissions
  • Process right-to-erasure requests
  • Review DTAC/DSPT evidence documentation

Section 04

Functional Requirements

📅 M1 — Online Booking Portal Must Have
  • FR-1.1Patient-facing booking interface accessible without login
  • FR-1.2Real-time availability calendar showing open appointment slots
  • FR-1.3Appointment type selection (in-person / telehealth)
  • FR-1.4Automated email confirmation sent on booking, rescheduling, and cancellation
  • FR-1.5Automated SMS reminder sent 24h and 1h before appointment
  • FR-1.6Patient self-service reschedule and cancellation (up to configurable cut-off time)
  • FR-1.7Admin calendar view: day/week/month with drag-to-reschedule
  • FR-1.8Blocked-time management for staff unavailability
🧑‍💻 M2 — Patient Portal Must Have
  • FR-2.1Secure patient account registration and login (email + password, MFA optional)
  • FR-2.2Dashboard showing upcoming and past appointments
  • FR-2.3Access to own clinical notes (view-only) released by clinician
  • FR-2.4Document library: download uploaded clinical files and results
  • FR-2.5Digital consent forms: display, acknowledge, and e-sign
  • FR-2.6Secure inbox: thread-based messaging with clinic staff
  • FR-2.7GDPR self-service: data export request and right-to-erasure request workflow
  • FR-2.8Notification preferences management (email/SMS on/off)
🎥 M3 — Telehealth Video Must Have
  • FR-3.1WebRTC-based video call via Daily.co, integrated into the platform (no external app)
  • FR-3.2Patient waiting room: patient waits until clinician admits them
  • FR-3.3Clinician dashboard: admit/reject waiting patient, end session
  • FR-3.4Mobile-responsive video UI (iOS Safari, Android Chrome)
  • FR-3.5Pre-call device check: camera, microphone, speaker test
  • FR-3.6Session routing via Daily.co EU region (no US infrastructure transit)
  • FR-3.7Session link auto-generated and sent to patient on booking
🗂 M4 — EHR Lite (SOAP Records) Must Have
  • FR-4.1Structured SOAP note template (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) per session
  • FR-4.2Free-text and structured field entry within each SOAP section
  • FR-4.3Treatment plan tracking: goals, progress markers, review dates
  • FR-4.4Document upload (PDF, JPEG, PNG) attached to patient record
  • FR-4.5Chronological patient timeline: all notes, documents, and sessions
  • FR-4.6Clinician-controlled visibility: choose which notes patient can see in portal
  • FR-4.7Note versioning: edits logged with timestamp and author
💳 M5 — Billing & Invoicing Must Have
  • FR-5.1Invoice generation per appointment or manually triggered
  • FR-5.2Invoice fields: service description, date, amount, VAT, patient details, clinic details
  • FR-5.3Invoice status tracking: Draft → Sent → Paid / Overdue
  • FR-5.4Manual payment recording (cash, BACS, insurance)
  • FR-5.5Stripe integration pre-wired (keys configurable, online payment activation-ready)
  • FR-5.6PDF invoice download and email delivery to patient
  • FR-5.7Billing summary view: outstanding, paid, and overdue totals
🔒 M6 — GDPR Compliance Layer Must Have
  • FR-6.1Role-based access control (RBAC): Patient, Clinician, Admin, IG Lead roles
  • FR-6.2Immutable audit log: every data read, write, export, and deletion timestamped with user ID
  • FR-6.3Consent management: granular consent records per patient with timestamp and version
  • FR-6.4Right-to-erasure workflow: request → IG review → anonymisation/deletion → confirmation log
  • FR-6.5Data minimisation: only fields necessary for clinical care are collected at registration
  • FR-6.6Session timeout after 15 minutes of inactivity for clinical staff accounts
  • FR-6.7Privacy notice presented and acknowledged at patient registration

Section 07

Compliance & Regulatory Requirements

  • 🔒
    UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018 Lawful basis for processing: legitimate interest (clinical care). Consent management, right-to-erasure workflow, data minimisation, privacy notice at registration, Subject Access Request support, DPA template provided to client.
  • 🏥
    NHS DTAC (Digital Technology Assessment Criteria) Completed DTAC self-assessment checklist submitted with Milestone 2. Evidence includes: data flow diagram, security controls documentation, GDPR measures, and clinical safety considerations.
  • 📋
    NHS DSPT (Data Security & Protection Toolkit) DSPT evidence summary delivered as part of DTAC add-on pack. Formatted for direct submission to NHS IG contact or CQC inspector.
  • 🌍
    Data Residency — UK Only All personally identifiable data physically stored in AWS eu-west-2 (London). Zero transit outside UK/EU at any point. Video sessions routed via Daily.co EU region only.
  • 📜
    CQC Inspection Readiness One-page data flow diagram with service-level encryption detail provided. Can be supplied directly to NHS IG contact or CQC inspector upon request. Audit logs exportable for inspection evidence.

Acceptance Criteria

Milestone 1 — Design & Architecture

  • ✓Wireframes provided for all 6 modules (Booking, Patient Portal, Telehealth, EHR, Billing, GDPR)
  • ✓UI mockups match agreed brand palette and design direction
  • ✓PostgreSQL schema covers all entities: patients, appointments, notes, documents, invoices, consent, audit
  • ✓Data flow diagram shows all services, regions, and encryption states
  • ✓Client has reviewed and approved all above before Milestone 1 payment released

Milestone 2 — Full Platform Delivery

  • ✓All user stories marked Must Have (US-01 to US-05, US-07 to US-14, US-16, US-17) are fully functional on production
  • ✓Patient can complete a booking, receive reminders, and join a telehealth session end-to-end without assistance
  • ✓Clinician can write, save, and view a SOAP note attached to a patient session
  • ✓Admin can generate an invoice and record a manual payment
  • ✓Audit log captures at least: login events, record reads, note saves, document uploads, and erasure requests
  • ✓All data confirmed physically hosted in AWS eu-west-2 (verified via AWS console)
  • ✓TLS certificate active and HTTPS enforced on harmonyphysio.co.uk
  • ✓DTAC self-assessment checklist, DPA template, and DSPT evidence summary delivered as PDF documents
  • ✓DNS records (A, MX, SPF, DKIM) configured and verified
  • ✓Platform passes basic accessibility check (WCAG 2.1 AA key criteria)
